# Umayan Marriage & Banquet Hall

**Bringing local celebrations closer to the digital world.**

A responsive business website developed for Umayan Marriage & Banquet Hall, a local event venue situated in a small village.

The project aims to give a traditionally offline business a professional digital identity, making it easier for people to discover the venue, explore its facilities and enquire about hosting their special occasions.

---

## 🌿 About Umayan

Every celebration deserves a beautiful beginning.

Located in a small village, Umayan Marriage & Banquet Hall provides a space for families and communities to come together for weddings, receptions and other memorable occasions.

While businesses in larger cities are increasingly embracing digital platforms, smaller local businesses often have limited online visibility.

This project is an attempt to bridge that gap.

By creating a dedicated online presence for Umayan, the goal is to make the venue more discoverable and accessible to people beyond its immediate surroundings.

**The idea is simple: geographical location should not limit digital visibility.**

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Establish a professional digital identity for a local business.
- Improve the venue's online discoverability.
- Showcase actual venue photographs and available facilities.
- Make customer enquiries easier and more accessible.
- Explore how technology can support the growth of small and local businesses.

## ✨ Features

- Elegant, responsive website design.
- Premium ivory and gold visual theme.
- Business introduction and venue information.
- Facilities showcase.
- Actual venue photography gallery.
- Customer enquiry interface.
- WhatsApp enquiry integration.

## 💡 The Bigger Picture

This project represents more than a frontend development exercise.

It explores how accessible digital technologies can help small businesses establish an online presence, reach potential customers and participate in the growing digital economy.

It is also an opportunity to understand the practical side of building technology for real-world users rather than developing applications purely for demonstration.
